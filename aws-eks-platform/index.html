Services

AWS account and EKS platform from scratch, as code

When you need it

You are starting a product or moving it to AWS and do not want to untangle click-built infrastructure a year later. Or you already have an account, but nobody can say what was created in it and why.

What you get

Account and network structure, EKS with Karpenter, IRSA, secrets in Secrets Manager or SOPS with KMS, GitOps deployment through Argo CD, observability from day one with Prometheus or Mimir, Loki and Grafana, alerting rules routed to service owners. Everything as Terraform/Terragrunt code in your repository, with a merge-request process your team uses to make changes itself. Documentation: repository layout, how to add a service, a database or a secret, how to upgrade. Handover: a joint code walkthrough and the first changes made by your team under my review.

Typical duration

A minimal platform for one environment: 3–5 weeks. Several environments with observability and GitOps: 6–10 weeks. Estimate after a first call.
